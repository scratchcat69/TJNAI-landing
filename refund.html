Refund Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What this covers

This policy is how TJNAI.ai LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company, handles refunds. It depends on what you bought.

  • Digital products are sold and delivered through Gumroad, which processes those payments. The PickleRecord Coach Court Kit is one example.
  • Services — consulting and custom work — are invoiced by us and paid through Stripe.

tjnai.ai itself does not charge your card. To ask for a refund, email info@tjnai.ai. This policy is part of our Terms of Service.

Digital downloads

If you bought a digital download and want a refund, email info@tjnai.ai within 14 days of the purchase. Include the product name and the email address you used at Gumroad checkout.

We review each request on its own. A request inside the window is not, by itself, an approval. We look at what was delivered and whether you already received the files.

If we approve a refund, it is processed through Gumroad. We do not send a separate payment outside Gumroad for a Gumroad purchase. Gumroad’s own processing rules affect when the money moves and how it shows up.

Services paid through Stripe

Refunds for consulting and custom work follow the signed agreement or the invoice for that work. If those documents address a refund, they control.

If they do not say how a refund works, prepaid amounts for work we have not yet performed may be refunded. Work we have already done is not refunded under this default.

Email info@tjnai.ai with the invoice number and what you want canceled or refunded. If a refund is due, it is returned through Stripe to the original payment method when Stripe can do that.

How to ask

Write to info@tjnai.ai. Tell us what you bought, when, and the email address or invoice tied to the payment. We will reply as soon as we reasonably can. This policy does not promise a reply within a set number of days.

Please email us before you open a payment dispute with your bank, Gumroad, or Stripe. A bank dispute and a refund request on the same charge can get in each other’s way.

Rights that stay in place

This policy does not take away a right the law gives you that a contract cannot waive, including a consumer right under North Carolina law.
